
# HealthBar Mod for Silksong

This mod lets you see boss and enemie health by creating a simple bar top of your screen.

There is also some hotkeys for you. By this hotkeys you can edit the layout size and the position of the healthbar on your screen


Hotkeys:
Hold Ctrl + ↑ to move the entire boss health bar component upward by 0.1 pixels.
Hold Ctrl + ↓ to move the entire boss health bar downward by 0.1 pixels.
Hold Ctrl + ← to move the boss health bar left by 0.1 pixels.
Hold Ctrl + → to move the entire boss health bar right by 0.1 pixels.
Hold Ctrl + Numpad + to increase the scale of the boss health bar by 0.1 (not recommended).
Hold Ctrl + Numpad - to decrease the scale of the entire boss health bar by 0.1 (not recommended).
Hold Ctrl + Non-Numpad + to increase the horizontal length of the boss health bar by 0.1.
Hold Ctrl + Non-Numpad - to decrease the horizontal length of the boss health bar by 0.1.
Hold Ctrl + [ to increase the vertical width of the boss health bar by 0.1.
Hold Ctrl + ] to decrease the vertical width of the boss health bar by 0.1.
The position of the health bar is refreshed in real time after each adjustment.
The same adjustments apply to regular enemies, but hold Alt instead of Ctrl.
Ctrl + Numpad 5: Reset the boss health bar.
Ctrl + Numpad 6: Reset the regular enemy health bar
