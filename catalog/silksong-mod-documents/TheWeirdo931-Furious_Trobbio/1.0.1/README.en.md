## Contents of this mod (Example):
* Main features
Disclaimer: This section has spoilers on the custom behavior of Furious Trobbio. If you don't want spoilers, please do not read this section.

This section references the wiki page to Tormented Trobbio and a link to that will be attached here. Furious Trobbio has 1500 health which is split across 3 phases. Each phase and its attack are listed below, and note that if an attack from the wiki doesn't appear here, that means its unchanged in the fight:

Phase 1:
    
---Glorious Glitter Bombs - An upgraded version of Tormented Trobbio's Baleful Bombs. In this version, each bombs behavior is unchanged but the fireworks are emitted in 8 directions instead of 4.
---Furious Fireworks - An upgraded version of Tormented Trobbio's Fell Fireworks. In the original version, Trobbio jumps into the air, shouting "Trobbio" before a giant x or + is emitted from his body and 5 firework objects explode around the stage. In this version, the fireworks are unchanged, but Furious Trobbio fires both the x and the + simultaneously. 

Phase 2 - Upon reaching 1100 health, Furious Trobbio will enter phase 2 and gain:
    
---Passionate Pyrotechnics - An upgraded version of Tormented Trobbio's Profane Pyrotechnics. In this version, the amount of fire pillars is increased from 7 to 10 and Trobbio will leap out of the floor on the 6th or 8th fire pillar randomly.

Phase 3 - Upon reaching 600 health, Furious Trobbio will enter phase 3 and gain:
    
---Wraithful Wave - An upgraded version of Passionate Pyrotechnics. In this version, Furious Trobbio activated all 7 fire pillars in a wave across the screen, leaving one off randomly as a place to dodge the screen-wide attack.
---Wild Whirlwind - An upgraded version of Woeful Whirlwind. In this version, Furious Trobbio will spawn 3 glitter bombs as it traverses the screen. These bombs will bounce around wildly before exploding in the same 8 directions from the Glorious Glitter Bomb Attack.
