## Trypophobia Friendly Glooms

This removes all the holes from the **Gargant Gloom** and **Gloomsac** enemies (they are late game if you haven't already seen them)


### Haven't heard of trypophobia before?
**It's the aversion to or fear of clusters of small holes or bumps.**

I found some people were looking for something like this so I made it :)<br>
