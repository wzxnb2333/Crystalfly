# ResurrectMinigames
A simple mod that adds back Lumble and Loddie, despite their unfortunate demises.
It also gives both of them 999 999 rosaries (which also reset on room reload).
The NPCs only get 999 999 rosaries after you acquire the exclusive items, making them unable to be missed.

# Manual Installation
Take `ResurrectMinigames.dll` out of `ResurrectMinigames.zip` and put it into `BepInEx\plugins`.

Done.
