# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- **Single Boss** mode: the Boss Rush button now opens a choice between Single Boss and Boss Rush. Single Boss fights
  one boss of your choice.
- **Difficulty** page: boss HP from 0.1x to 5.0x, extra damage taken (+1 to +4 masks), and one-hit death.
- Translated into every language the game supports, using the game's own names for bosses, items and abilities.
- Fixed: the second Savage Beastfly fight getting stuck attacking a corner.
- Fixed: Groal the Great coming out before the enemy waves were cleared.
- Fixed: Nyleth's platform sinking during her intro after a fall into the spikes.
- More pre-boss skips, including the Unravelled's first corpse.
- Long text in the menus shrinks to fit.

- First release.
