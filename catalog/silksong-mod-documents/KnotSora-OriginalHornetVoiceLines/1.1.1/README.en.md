# Original Hornet Voice Lines

Have you ever wanted to hear the good old Hornet voice lines from the original Hollow Knight when playing Silksong?

Did you ever want Hornet to tell you to "Get Gud" when you die?

If the answer to these questions is "Yes" then this is the mod for you!

Featuring classics such as:
- Shaw
- Adino
- Hegale
- And more!

## Customize your experience!

The config allows you to change the chance a voice line will be triggered, which voice lines can play and the volume at which they will be played.
