# Custom Title Cards
This mod allows the player to replace all title
cards. This means you can change the intro text for
bosses, areas, and NPCs.

To modify a title card, open the Bepinex menu (F1).
Title cards are split into individual categories, 
with their default value displayed as the title. Each 
title card is further split into 3 fields:
- Field 1, the super line, is the top line and is 
typically a smaller font
- Field 2, the main line, is the middle line and uses
the largest font
- Field 3, the sub line, is the bottom line and often
the smallest font

Not all title cards use all 3 lines. You will have to 
experiment to figure out which title cards use which 
lines.
