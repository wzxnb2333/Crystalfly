# All Tools Unlockable 

Allows all tools to be unlocked in normal gameplay. Tools will be given to the player when certain conditions are met and either you acquire a tool or Hornet is close enough to a bench for the rest prompt to appear (includes loading a save where Hornet is sitting on a bench). Each tool unlock can be enabled individually in the plugin's config file; all unlocks are disabled by default. Tool downgrades that have been re-unlocked will not replace their upgrades. 

### Tools unlocked by this mod will NOT be removed from your inventory when the mod is disabled. This will have a permanent effect on your save file.

##

### With all config settings enabled... 

- The other Silkshots will be unlocked when one is acquired. 
- The Dead Bug's Purse or Shell Satchel will be unlocked when the other is acquired. 
- The Curveclaw (downgrade) will be re-unlocked when the Curvesickle is acquired. 
- The Druid's Eye (downgrade) will be re-unlocked when the Druid's Eyes are acquired. 
- The Claw Mirror (downgrade) will be re-unlocked when the Claw Mirrors are acquired. 
- The Snare Setter will be re-unlocked when you enter Act 3. 
- The Needle Phial will be re-unlocked when the Plasmium Gland is acquired.
