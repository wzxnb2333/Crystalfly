Quick disclaimer: this mod has no real effect on the story or progression, so nothing is broken here if you are worried about that! This can be added to an already existing save without any problems as I have tested on my own saves.


It would also be greatly appreciated for some word of mouth to get this mod spread around
 
Are you like me and from the moment you saw the twelfth architect you immediately decided this was one of your favorite characters?

Were you sad  when you bought all the items from the and they were dead by the next time you went to the workshop?

Did you learn this when you go to the workshop after starting act 3 and got greeted with that grisly sight?

Did you wish it didn't have to be this way?

Simply put, this mod forcibly overrides the save flags that enable their death to always be false, and edits the dialogue to match this change. Yes, even in act 3 they’ll still be alive.

If they are already dead on your save, this will undo it, so this works on existing saves.

This mod also adds extra dialogue for the 12th architect. (if you download v1.1.1 or later)
