# The Gamemodes

## Bound Soul
- The basic gamemode, In it allows the triggering of the pantheon bindings

## SteelBound
- The steel version of Bound Soul, In it death is permanent (and steelsoul-exclusive features are enabled)

## GlassBound

# The Pantheon Bindings

Press H while in any of the 3 new gamemodes to open the bindings Menu

## The Needle Binding:
- Reduces Damage Dealt by Needle and Needle Arts to 80%
- Damage Dealt by Needle or Needle Arts can't exceed 13

## The Silk Binding:
- Reduces Notches of Silk needed to Bind from 9 to 4
- Reduces Max Silk Notches to 4
- Caps Passive Silk Gain to 1
- Bind only heals back 1 Mask

## The Mask Binding:
- Reduces Max Masks to 4.

## The Tool Binding
- Remove ability to equip a Silk Skill or Tool
