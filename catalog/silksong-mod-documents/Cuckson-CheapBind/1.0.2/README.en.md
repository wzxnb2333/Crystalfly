Binding is now cheaper by default, costing only 3 silk. If you have 3 silk hearts that means it's just on a timer!

This will not work while cursed, or under any effects that will cause you to have no natural silk, naturally.

Currently this mod will still cost the 9 silk to bind, but you can set the amount required to bind at any amount.

You can customize this in your BepInEx/Config folder!

### - Dependencies
BepInEx-BepInExPack_Silksong-5.4.2303

Check out [BuffBind](https://thunderstore.io/c/hollow-knight-silksong/p/Cuckson/BuffBind/)!
