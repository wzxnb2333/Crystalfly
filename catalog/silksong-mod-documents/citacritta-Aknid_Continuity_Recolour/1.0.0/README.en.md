## Aknid Continuity Recolour

This recolours the **Aknid** enemy to better fit the current continuity of Silksong. 


### Ever wondered why the Aknid has such out of place colours?

In Silksong's development, there was an area planned called **Cloverland** (or **Aspid**).<br>
Parts of it were repurposed for Verdania, and others scrapped. Of this, there remain a collection of enemies in the files that would have been its inhabitants. They share the teal and yellow colour palette.<br>
The only enemy of this group that gets used in the final game is the Aknid, now placed in an area it wasn't designed to fit in. <br>

Hence why it has strange colours :D
