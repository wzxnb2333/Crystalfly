# Silksong Voidsong mod
## About
This mod replaces pretty much every enemy with their threaded variants, giving even bosses the attacks from start of game, also i've used ai in making of this mod, as a beginner i didnt have much experience in modding, but not vibecoded, just ai-assisted process of creation :D
