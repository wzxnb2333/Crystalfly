Core Mechanics: Taunt Key + Directional Input for Crest Swapping

Crest swapping occurs after completing the input action, with real-time visual effects updating in the top-left corner.

Short Press Taunt + Direction:

Up:

Short press: Toolmaster (Architect)

Short/long press after initial swap: Queen

Down:

Short press: Spell (Shaman)

Short/long press after initial swap: Hunter

Left:

Short press: Reaper

Short/long press after initial swap: Warrior (Beast)

Right:

Short press: Wanderer

Short/long press after initial swap: Witch

Long Press Taunt (0.2s hold) + Direction:

Up:

Long press: QueenCrest (Queen’s Crest)

Short/long press after initial swap: Toolmaster

Down:

Long press: Hunter

Short/long press after initial swap: Spell

Left:

Long press: Warrior (Beast)

Short/long press after initial swap: Reaper

Right:

Long press: Witch

Short/long press after initial swap: Wanderer

Extended Long Press Mechanics:

Holding Taunt for 3 seconds cycles to Cursed Crest.

Holding Cursed Crest for another 3 seconds cycles to Cloakless Crest.

Gameplay Parameters:

Invincibility Frames: 0.1 seconds during crest swap.

Cooldown: 0.3 seconds between swaps.

Soul Cost:

0 for crest swapping (but 1 Soul for the taunt action itself).

Bug Fixes:

Fixed: Sitting on benches now properly restocks all crest items (not just one).

Fixed: Character freeze when swapping crests during Dash Slash animation.

Keybinding Note:

Mod requires restart after changing keybindings to apply changes.
