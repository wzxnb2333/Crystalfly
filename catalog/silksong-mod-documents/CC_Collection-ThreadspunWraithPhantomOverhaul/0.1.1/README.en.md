# Threadspun Wraith

A massive overhaul of the Phantom boss fight in Hollow Knight: Silksong. The Threadspun Wraith can be challenged in the Exhaust Organ at any time, as long as Phantom has not been defeated in the current save.

Compatibility with other mods, including Godhome mods, has not been tested. Please note that the arena gates may not open after defeating the boss; quitting to menu in this position should place Hornet on your last bench with Cross Stitch equipped.

Requires AssetHelper, FsmUtil, and BepInEx
