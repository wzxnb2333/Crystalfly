# Forsaken Sentinels
Adds a duo fight against Phantom and Second Sentinel. To access the fight, visit the Exhaust Organ after waking Second Sentinel with the Cogwork Heart and *before* defeating Phantom.

The mod makes small changes to the Exhaust Organ, as well as modifying the intro, phase change, and end of the boss fight.

~It is known that defeating Second Sentinel before Phantom stops the background music for the remainder of the fight. This may or may not be fixed in the future.~
