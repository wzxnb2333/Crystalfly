# ZaZa in Bellhome

This mod allows to take ZaZa to your bellhome after defeating Lost Garmond in Act 3.
After interacting with ZaZa next to Garmonds corpse you can take him with you. When you go to your Bellhome he will be on the floor net to your bed.

To change the position of ZaZa do the following steps:
1. Navigate to your Silksong Game Files Folder
2. Open the BepInEx folder
3. Open the config folder
4. Open com.fabbeyond.zaza_in_bellhome.cfg
5. Change the zazaPosition value in the correct savefile category
6. Restart your game

Positions:
0: On the floor next to the bed
1: On the ledge above your bed
