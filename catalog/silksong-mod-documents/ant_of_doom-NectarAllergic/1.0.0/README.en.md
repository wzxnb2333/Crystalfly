# Silksong-Nectar-Allergic-Mod
A hilarious and useless Silksong mod. It adds a new feature: if Hornet buys a nectar from Creige in Halfway Home, she'll pass out.
This may be a good utility for certain moments in YouTube videos, I guess.
It can be configured quite a bit.

# Manual Installation
Take `NectarAllergic.dll` out of `NectarAllergic.zip` and put it into `BepInEx\plugins`.

Take the folder `Plugins` out of `silksong_modding-AssetHelper-1.3.0.zip` and put it into `BepInEx\plugins`.

Download all the dependencies of AssetHelper.

Take `Silksong.I18N.dll` out of `silksong_modding-I18N-1.0.2.zip` and put it into `BepInEx\plugins`.

Take the folder `patchers` out of `MonoDetour-MonoDetour_BepInEx_5-0.7.12.zip` and put it into `BepInEx\patchers`.

Take the folder `core` out of `MonoDetour-MonoDetour-0.7.13.zip` and put it into `BepInEx\core`.

Done.

# Config
It's called `com.sealofapproval.nectarallergic.cfg` and has 3 settings.

One that toggles whether Hornet shall be damaged (she'll have 1 hp left) after drinking nectar,

one that toggles whether Hornet shall outright die,

and one that changes the sound effects' volume (because I've adjusted them according to what I'm used to).

To change something, just replace the value in the last line of each "paragraph" with an another value.

Showcase of settings: https://youtu.be/jEpwU75WspA
