# Nuclear Bomb

Drops a nuke when you press N, can be changed in the config.

The speed of the explosion and the volume of the sounds can also be changed.

The nuke deals 999 damage to anything it touches (except for hornet, of course).



The effects are made using assets from the original Hollow Knight, with the shaders intentionally broken. May look weird or not work at all in some rooms.





## Manual Install

Like with most other mods, unzip the file, and copy the Increase Bomb.dll file into your Hollow Knight Silksong\\BepInEx\\plugins folder.
