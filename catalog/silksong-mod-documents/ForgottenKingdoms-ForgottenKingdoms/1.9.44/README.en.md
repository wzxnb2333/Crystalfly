## **Forgotten Kingdoms**



Explore the kingdom of Kimera and uncover the truth behind the curse of the sand.

Journey through the three forgotten dream worlds of Whispering Dunes, discover hidden areas, secrets, bosses, and multiple endings.



Forgotten Kingdoms is currently in beta. Content is not complete, but the mod is stable and playable.



To begin your journey, interact with the Pilgrim in Surface (Nameless Town).
