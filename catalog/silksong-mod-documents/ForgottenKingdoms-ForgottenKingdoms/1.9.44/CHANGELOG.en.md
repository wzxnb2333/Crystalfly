* 
* \* Added a brand-new area: \*\*City of Duality\*\*.

* \## Gameplay
* 
* \* Silk Soar can now be used in every area.
* \* Players are no longer forced to use Hornet's initial Crest in \*\*Distant Memories\*\*.
* \* Adjusted several challenges in \*\*Distant Memories\*\*.
* \* Reduced the size of oversized background objects in several areas.
* 
* \## Bug Fixes \& Improvements
* 
* \* Fixed an issue where players could move during the Roar Lock animation at the beginning and end of the first gauntlet.
* \* Fixed an issue where the arena gate would sometimes fail to open.
* \* Reduced how high the \*\*Driznarga\*\* enemies jump in the first \*\*Whispering Dunes\*\* gauntlet.
* \* Fixed several hitboxes in \*\*Distant Memories\*\*.
* \* Fixed multiple collision issues in \*\*Distant Memories\*\*.
* \* Fixed the animation when entering the \*\*Myrax\*\* arena.
* \* Fixed an issue where the second \*\*Duality Key\*\* OST would break after collecting the \*\*Scroll of Duality\*\*.
* \* Fixed several sprite layering issues caused by identical Z offsets across multiple areas. Some rare cases may still occur, so please report them on our Discord server.
* \* Fixed various NPC dialogue issues.
* \* Reduced the red lighting intensity throughout \*\*Crimson Ways\*\*.
* 
* \## Item Changes
* 
* \* \*\*Scroll of Fear\*\* has been renamed to \*\*Scroll of Nightmares\*\*.
* \* \*\*Scroll of Bravery\*\* has been renamed to \*\*Scroll of Serenity\*\*.
