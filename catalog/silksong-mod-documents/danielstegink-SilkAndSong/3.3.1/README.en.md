# Silk & Song
This mod adds an RPG-style level-up mechanic to 
Silksong. By defeating enemies and completing quests, 
the player can accumulate XP which increases Hornet's 
level. Levels in turn increase Hornet's abilities in 
various areas.

A new Wish, Silk & Song, will be added to Hornet's 
list. It shows her current level, how much XP she 
needs for the next level, and what perks she gets for 
her current level.

## Level-up Guide
### Enemies
Enemies are divided into 3 groups: enemies, 
minibosses and bosses.
- Enemies are generic enemies that give 1 XP. 
- Minibosses are enemies that are significantly 
stronger than regular enemies, but aren't quite 
bosses. They give 5 XP. 
- Bosses are the strongest enemies and give 10 XP. 

The XP is multiplied by what Act the enemy first 
appears in (with some exceptions), so Bell Beast gives
10 XP, Cogwork Dancers give 20, and Bell Eater gives 
30. 

Some enemies, such as muckmaggots, don't give XP due 
to their low difficulty level.

### Wishes
Wishes give significantly more XP than enemies, due to their 
rarity, how long they take to complete, and how RPGs 
traditionally give XP.

Wishes are divided much the same way as enemies. They 
have 3 levels of difficulty giving 25, 50 and 100 XP, 
which is then multiplied by the Act in which you would 
most likely complete the given Wish. For example, the 
Flintbeetles Wish gives 25 XP, but Great Taste of 
Pharloom gives 200.

Most Wishes can only be completed once, but Wishes 
that can be completed multiple times, such as Delivery
Wishes, count for each time they were completed.

### XP Requirements
Levelling up is a slow process; each level requires 
significantly more XP than the last. You can reach 
level 1 as early as the first boss, but you probably 
won't reach level 10 until Act 3. 

You can customize this via the Bepinex menu (F1).

## Perks
At each level, Hornet gets the following bonuses:
- 2.5% bonus in needle damage
- 4.17% bonus in spell/tool damage
- 0.00625 bonus in masks regenerated per second
- 0.009 bonus in Silk regenerated per second

So at level 4, Hornet will have:
- +10% needle damage
- +16.67% spell/tool damage
- 1 Mask regenerated every 40 seconds
- 1 Silk regenerated every 27.5 seconds
