# The Void Crest
* Automatically unlocked (for now)
* Darkens the world around You (Literally) (Can be turned off in configurations if it makes it too hard to see)

# Binding
* Binding with Void Crest **will no longer heal You**
* You are **Invincible** while binding
* Being hit while binding will now deal **retaliation damage** back against the enemy scaling off Your *needle's damage*
  * (Retaliation Damage = Needle Damage * 2, Doesn't generate silk on hit)
* Every time You deal retaliation damage while binding, Gain 1 Voidmass

# Voidmass
* Has a black spool below the silk spool to keep count of it, goes up to a max of **10**
* Every time You use a Silk Skill with at least 1 Voidmass; consume 1 Voidmass to voidify and double the damage of Your Silk Skill
* Pressing Bind + Up will consume **ALL** Your Voidmass to unleash a powerful attack that scales in strength based off Voidmass consumed
* After executing the Bind + Up attack, heal masks equal to half of the Voidmass consumed (Rounded down)
* Resting at a bench sets Voidmass to 0 (for obvious reasons)

# Void Slots
* This crest has only Void Slots, a Void Slot can take **any tool regardless of it's color**
  * Notes: When using tools that need to be triggered by a binding (I.e, red tools) it can not hold more than 1 tool per binding slot

# Moveset
* Currently just witch's moveset, but without a typical Dash Attack
* Instead of a Dash Attack, You can now dash through enemies to deal damage to them without taking damage Yourself, scales off Needle Damage
  * (Dash Damage = Needle Damage * 0.5, Doesn't generate silk on hit)

# Unique Tool Synergies
* Injector band
  * This nerfs the bind by making binding last shorter
  * To compensate, You gain 1 additional Voidmass when hit while binding
  * Also increases the binding retaliation damage by 50% 
    * (Additive, making it 250% of nail damage, not 300%)

* Multibinder 
  * Now no longers makes You bind twice
  * Whenever You deal damage from either dashing through an enemy or binding retaliation, It generates silk like normal needle attacks do
  * Also increases the binding retaliation damage by 50% 
    * (Additive, making it 250% of nail damage, not 300%)

* Warding Bell 
  * Now is fully useless due to You ignoring damage from binding anyways
  * So it just gives another +100% binding retaliation damage

* Silkspeed Anklets 
  * Now also increase dash damage by 50% 
    * (Additive, making it 100% of nail damage, not 75%)

* Weighted Belt
  * Now also increase dash damage by 75% 
    * (Additive, making it 125% of nail damage)

* Plasmium Phial
  * Now no longer grants blue masks, but instead gives You 1 voidmass when You use it

# Configuration
* After booting the game up once with this mod loaded, if you go to gamefolder/BepInEx/config and open io.dervorce.voidcrest.cfg, you will find many configurations, including changing nearly all the numbers mentioned here, and even changing the base crest
