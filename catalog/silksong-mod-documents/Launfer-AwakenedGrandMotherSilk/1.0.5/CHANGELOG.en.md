
## v1.0.5
- Reduced the length of all Rubble attacks so they trap the player less. This also decreases the length of stagger transition phase in the middle of the fight.
- Rebalanced the HP of the 5th and 6th phase. Total health is similar, but the boss will spend slightly longer in the final phase.
- Widened the gaps of certain pin attacks, making them a bit easier to dodge if Hornet is in the air.
- Increased the telegprah length of the pins slightly.
- Removed the pin attack variation that swipes 3 times with no stomp in between, telegraphs could be concealed if they overlapped prior swipes.
- Slightly reduced the chance for the Spike Floors to be chosen over the Rubble attacks in the second half of the fight.
- Added damage reduction while the boss is screaming to perform her Silk Nuke barrage in the final phase of the fight.
- Further increased the volume of the Silk Orb summon audio to be heard better.

## v1.0.4
- Added Traditional Chinese localisation.
- Increased the HP of most phases, the total increase is about 250.
- Updated the sprites of the Silk Orb appearing and hitting the ground to animate slightly better.
- Slightly reduced the length of the shield animation that plays during the Web Barrage to be better timed with GMS ending her scream animation.

## v1.0.3
- Fixed a bug that prevented GMS's wait time from being updated correctly during her downtime in between attacks.
- Edited a few more wait time variables to speed up certain moves, overall the fight should feel a bit faster.
- Fixed a bug that sometimes caused the extra Web Strand in the final phase to not be ready to fire if the boss used the attack consecutive times.
- Removed some info logging messages.

## v1.0.2
- Altered the HP for some phases, most notably increased the HP of the final phase. Slight overall buff to total HP.
- Increased the size of the Silk Orb explosion slightly.
- Widened the gaps in the pin variation that pincers Hornet from both sides, making it a bit easier to dodge.	

## v1.0.1
- Reduced the HP for first half phases slightly, increased the HP of the final phase slightly.
- Increased the volume of the Silk Orb summon audio to be heard better.
- In the second half of the fight the chance to do a double Claw Dash is now changed from 50% to 75%.
